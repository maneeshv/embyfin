Operator recovery

Keep the full repository runbook, deployment identifiers, and operator credentials outside D1.

Before restoring

Use the Cloudflare control plane to enable the external maintenance gate, stop schedules, and destroy the processor. Download the latest completed SQL export and manifest from the private backup bucket; verify its checksum and age.

Restore into isolation

Import into a new D1 database. Run ops/recover.sql to disable restored viewers and grants and invalidate all leases. Run ops/verify-restore.sql and compare catalog counts, favorites, and progress. Reconcile current access separately; a backup cannot prove a viewer was not revoked later.

Verify before cutover

Reapprove current users, test disjoint library grants and revoked access, then switch the D1 binding while maintenance remains enabled. Reopen only after verification. Resume approved libraries and take a fresh backup.

Recovery targets

Provisional maximum data loss: 24 hours. Recovery target: 4 hours after operator action. Measure both in a drill while the app is unavailable and verify external stale-backup alert delivery.

Originals are not duplicated. Posters are regenerable. Same-account R2 backups do not cover account-wide loss. The complete procedure is in docs/RECOVERY.md.
